The Model City Platform

Seven pillars.
One blueprint.

A Model City is not built on slogans. It is built on pillars — specific, measurable commitments that transform aspiration into architecture. These are the foundations of Stonecrest's future.

I

The Church as the City's Heartbeat

Stonecrest's churches will be empowered — not merely tolerated — as the central institution of city life. Pastoral voices will have a formal seat at the table. Faith-based organizations will be the city's partners in development, wellness, education, and economic empowerment.

II

Superior Schools

Every child in Stonecrest will have access to an education that prepares them for a life — not just a job. We will partner with faith-based providers, businesses, and community leaders to elevate standards and restore the conviction that education is a sacred calling.

III

Safe Streets — No Crime

A holy city is a safe city. When families are strong, when young men have purpose, when the church is empowered — crime withers. Stonecrest will pursue public safety through both accountable law enforcement and deep community investment.

IV

Soaring Property Values

When a city is safe, its schools excellent, its character strong, and its community cohesive — property values rise. That is not a political promise. That is an economic certainty. Every pillar of this vision makes Stonecrest one of the most desirable addresses in metropolitan Atlanta.

V

Great Parks & Public Spaces

A city's parks are its living rooms. Stonecrest will invest in green spaces, recreational facilities, and community gathering places that reflect the dignity of the people who call this city home. Places families want to be. Places the community is proud of.

VI

Kingdom-Aligned Commerce

Stonecrest's commercial corridors will attract businesses that share our values — enterprises that treat employees with dignity, invest in the community, and operate with integrity. We will build a local economy where residents own a stake — not just a zip code.

VII

Fine People Who Know Who They Are

Above all — a Model City is made of model citizens. Stonecrest will invest in its people: their character, their sense of identity, their dignity, and their destiny. The entire Wise Men Group ecosystem becomes the city's investment in its own people.

The Flagship Vision

Zion's Black Wall Street.

In 1921, Black Wall Street in Tulsa, Oklahoma was the most prosperous Black community in America — and it was destroyed in a single night by those who feared what Black excellence could build. Over a century later, Stonecrest will finish what Tulsa started.

On the vacant land directly in front of Stonecrest Mall — sitting on Interstate 20, visible to the world — King Smith proposes the most ambitious development in DeKalb County history: Zion's Black Wall Street. A mixed-use destination built by and for the Black community, catered to Black excellence, and designed to draw visitors from across metropolitan Atlanta and beyond.

Grand Amphitheater

Outdoor concerts, orchestra, comedy, and major events — thousands of seats under the stars.

The Promenade

Open-air boutiques, shops, and restaurants — a walkable celebration of Black commerce and culture.

Entertainment Complex

TopGolf-style driving range, Andretti's-style racing, games for kids and adults — a destination for families.

Residences & Hotel

Luxury apartments, condominiums, and a boutique hotel — live, stay, and thrive in Zion.

Food Hall & Dining

A curated food hall celebrating Black culinary culture, plus fine dining and outdoor terraces.

Signature Lighting

A landmark visible from I-20 — as breathtaking at night as by day. Zion will announce itself to the world.

Developers and investors interested in Zion's Black Wall Street are invited to contact the campaign directly. This vision is moving forward.
